[[Session 2 - Zanzer Tem's Dungeon 2]]

Axel would eventually betray the party, having been working for Zanzer Tem to prevent their escape, and met his end in the salt mines. The rest of the group fought through the dungeon, making their way to the exit. Fighting a low level wizard enchanted to look like Zanzer, they prevailed and were free of the dungeon and salt mine.  From there they chose to delve into [[Stonefast]].
