Our adventure began in Zanzer Tem’s Dungeon, a wizard’s tower and salt mine located on the northwest tip of the [[Dragonspine Mountains]], where it meets the [[Khargast Mountains|Khargast Mountain Range]]

A group of adventurers, [[Revon|Revon Hoxley]], [[Ember]], [[Drafous]], and [[Axel|Axel Siegfried]] awoke captured, and locked in a jail cell together.  Upon conferring with one another, they realized they’d all been captured by Zanzer Tem, a [[Damarian]] wizard of some renown, whom several had sought out for assistance in their journeys.  Now they had one goal in mind.  Escape.

The jailer, Jarol, was tricked into opening the bars, and was overwhelmed by the group.  They slew him, but had mercy on [[Snivel]] the goblin, who tagged along with the group.  Making their way out of the dungeon, they also met up with [[Petra Pruz Kahoki|Petra Kahoki]], Elven Princess of the [[Daelen Wood]]
