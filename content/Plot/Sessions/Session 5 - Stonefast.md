[[Session 4 - Zanzer Tem's Dungeon 4, start of Stonefast]]

Slowly the quintet made their way to the center of the stronghold. Along the way they learned the history of [[Stonefast]], and what happened to it's people. At the heart of the fortress, they encountered Emro, a young white dragon who had been trapped there by Tem.  Despite an initially hostile meeting, peace was found, and the players helped the dragon to escape past Tem’s illusions which kept him there.  Equipped from the treasures of the stronghold, the players exited the stronghold into the sunlight, looking up a path leading to the tower of Zanzer Tem.
