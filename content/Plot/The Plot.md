# The Damarian Academy
Rumors of attacks on the Damarian Academy will reach the players. They go to investigate, and restore the portal so that other wizards may appear.  Many Damarian Wizards will be dead, and the Evoden Wizards will be implicated.  This is all part of the plan of the Landiss.  Those loyal to the secret cause were aware of the impending attack and spared.  Those who were not were slain.  The evidence against the Evoden wizards was to grant an excuse to destroy their stronghold, as they are the greatest direct threat to the Landiss Plan.
# The Ocean Domain
Entering the underwater palace, shutting the gates behind them before the angry mob catches them, the players will be thrust into darkness. Darkvision will not work, and light sources are so dampened they only grant the space the are in dim light, and cannot be seen from adjacent squares. They will bump into a halfling who will shriek and run off into the darkness. If grappled, the halfling will bite, hard, and roll an escape check (acrobatics) that WILL beat whomever's fortitude, escape, and dissapear into the dark.

While trying to locate one another in the dark, the players will become aware of a soft music playing, seeming to come from the walls themselves. Once out of the entryway, and the sphere of darkness (only one way to go) the players can regroup, and make their way through the palace. The music will be persistent, and gently growing louder. Cue a simple battle against some automated cleaning implements (use animated weapons), and the subsequent short rest, the players WILL notice they the music is in their brains, and they keep catching themselves humming along, or swaying with the music. THIS IS BAD….

Making their way through the palace, they will have 2-4 more encounters 2 moderate, 2 easy, with each short rest taken increasing the will penalty which they do not know is there.

Entering the throne room, they will be greeted with the spectacle of a full on ball. The guests present are of all races, dressed in rags that could be anything from sailor and pirate outfits to nobility. Insight of 10 makes it clear they are other shipwrecked passengers, though it's difficult to impossible to tell how long they've been here. Sitting atop a throne at the end of the ballroom is Azemar, the Bored Tyrant. He is a Vampire Wizard, who located this place 180 ish years ago. He has been using the magical whirlpool controlled by this civilization to sink ship, bring the passengers in for his amusement (and fresh food), while feeding them the moss which keeps them sustained and extra pliable. This is also amplified exponentially by the music box, which is a two part artefact. The internal music box is a focus which can be used by any arcane class, and will be detailed out elsewhere. The external part it is plugged into is an amplifier, which channels the smaller music box to enhance ritual casting in it's vicinity, granting a +5,10,or 15 to rituals cast by an attuned user while it is playing.

When playing Azemar, note he is bored. Like so bored that taking over a magical undersea civilization to try to find something to do bored. He has the means to leave whenever he wants, but doesn't know what else to do instead, and the food supply is steady here. To pass the time, he makes his "subjects" do anything he can think of to amuse him, whether it be dance, fight to the death, commit suicide, fornicate, eat one another, or anything he can think of. Right now they are having a ball. He has little desire to fight the PC's, and will use the innocents in the ballroom to stand in their way or act as shields for him, as well as use his rechargable mass suggestion ability to force the PC's to join into the "festivities". Should the PC's manage to resist, or once they break free, he will enlist the hardiest of warriors in the ballroom to engage them. As that clearly starts to fail, or better yet, should the PC's manage to stop the music box, he will flee into the adjacent chamber via gaseous form, through a crack in the wall. The players can seal the chamber with him inside, leaving him there to be entombed, or can break open the crack and fight him head on, as well as just leave him and flee.

His personal chambers lie beyond the throne room, which while locked, can easily be picked or broken into. Inside there is treasure, which will be some gear upgrade items, a few wonderous items, and most importantly Arduin's trinket. This is their escape. Further back is the exit, a large underwater lake with Arduin's vessel floating in it, though no obvious way out. The trinket will allow them to use the ship though, and once they manage to navigate through the undersea passage and surface, will get prompted for the chance to attune.

# The Landiss Plan
Upon reaching the Landiss Homebase, the heroes are welcomed in. Few others have answered the call, and those sent on the missions to help have yet to return, and the wizards fear the worst. The Landiss are in need of 5 great gems from across the lands, to be able to create powerful artefacts that will harness magick itself, bring the land into balance, and end the blight and undead uprising that has plagued the west. They have rumors with the location of 1 such gemstones already, and are working to locate the other 4, with help from emisarries from the fey.  If the players do not have linked portal yet, for fucks sake give it to them.

There are higher ups from all several wizard factions present (several of whom will be known to the PC's given their own affiliations, but all of whom are Landiss infiltrators), endorsing the Landiss plan, and encouraging the PC's to retrieve the stones. Make names for the people, and engrain them, as meeting members of the factions across the land will hint that the officials haven't been heard from in a while (hinting at the defection).

Pieces will be collected in order below:
# The heart stones
Kahlan's Peak (lone mountain in plains east of moonglade) - Pearl. Emro will be here, and show his grattitude for the players. He will let the players take the pearl heartstone, with a promise to return it, and give the players each a golden band with 3 pearls inlaid. He tells them that the pearls are magical, and will protect them from fire. They will activate 3 times only when needed, and will grant protection for 1 hour. The pearls do not radiate magic, and cannot be detected with arcana, but the dragon swears that they will save the players some day.

Should they discuss their plan, Emro will reveal that the plan sounds like it might work, as the pearl, and the other gems in question, are considered heart stones, which have sat in ley lines of the land and become tied to it. They are great catalysts of magick, and will allow rituals to be used on a world scale.

# Return of the Players
The players should be epic by the time they return. The players will arrive at Kahlens Peak, and Emro will be able to catch them up to what has happened. He will allow the players to live, declaring his letting them live as squaring their debt. He willalso explain the abyss stones were actually the shell of Tiamat, and have been bound to the heart stones, to make the orbs of dragonkind. He hates the players for their ignorance, but does not fault them. Lastly, he will send the landiss wizard back to the abyss to be dealt with by Tiamat personally, and take the players involvement to his grave. Upon his dissapearance through the portal, everything for the wizard will dissapear from all memory through the erasure spell.

# Revenge of the Players and Cleaning up their mess
The Landiss have enslaved dragons, and are killing wizards and mundane alike. The land is literally burning in places. War, true terrible war of magic has broken out. The only choice now is to end the Wizards War, by destroying the factions responsible. The Epic Campaign will be them Killing the wizards responsible (who are all pretty fucking mad with power, and extremely dangerous)

# The Finale and Epilogue
The Landiss Wizards have moved from Korad-Tir to the jungle temple.  The players will fight their way there, finally confronting an Epic Zanzer.  As the fight turns against him, he will open a portal to try to summon a planar ally.  Many will come through, pissed about the assault on Tiamat, and cast a ritual that will destroy the world by burning it to ash.  The PC's, and the allies they've made, will work to counter the ritual, which will limit the destruction so the world can survive.  The area around the temple becomes the Deadlands in future campaigns.  The players can then ascend to their epic destinies.

The orbs of dragonkind will be lost, buried and hidden away. They will resurface of course from time to time, but will come into play when Tiamat returns to exact her revenge.
