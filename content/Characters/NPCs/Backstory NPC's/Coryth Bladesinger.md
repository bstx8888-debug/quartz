# Coryth Bladesinger

Coryth Bladesinger is an Autumn Eladrin, a master of the swordmage arts of the White Lotus, and a follower in the footsteps of the great eladrin warrior, The Rose King.

He migrated from the Feywild to Zerith and joined a fledgling wizard guild focused on harmony between magic and blade. Refusing a leadership position, he instead shared his teachings freely. Over time, the guild grew and became known as the **[[White Lotus|White Lotus Academy]]**.

The academy welcomed members of all races and any magic users who wished to learn.

---

## The Fall of the White Lotus

Such openness came at a cost.

A growing number of students began bargaining for power with demons and devils—the path of the Warlock. What began as isolated choices became a widespread practice.

The consequences came too late.

The academy descended into chaos, erupting into a fiery and bloody civil war. The conflict ended in the death of the headmaster and most of the original staff.

From the ashes rose a new order: the **[[Pyracanth]]**.

They reshaped the academy into a recruitment and indoctrination center, serving their Balor Lord, [[Marduk]]. Tieflings were born in increasing numbers, raised and trained within the academy.

At the age of 21, each would accept Marduk’s demonic gift, joining a growing warlock army under his command.

---

## Ember

One such Tiefling was [[Ember]].

Unlike the others, Ember never felt at peace with the life she was born into. She questioned the path set before her.

Coryth saw this.

As one of the last masters of the old White Lotus ways, he took Ember—and a select few others—under his wing. He taught them the ancient discipline of the bladesinger: a fusion of martial precision and magical control.

> “Ages ago, there were legions of us knights.”

He would often say this before recounting stories of a bygone era.

Ember proved a natural—gifted with both blade and magic.

But one by one, Coryth’s students abandoned his teachings, choosing instead the power offered by [[Marduk]]. Each loss weighed heavily on him.

Ember swore she would not follow that path.

---

## Betrayal

One of Ember’s peers, the ambitious warlock [[Kairlu]], saw her hesitation as weakness.

With Ember’s 19th birthday approaching, he chose ambition over loyalty.

He betrayed her.

By exposing her resistance, he ensured her death sentence—while securing his own rise within the [[Pyracanth]].

Ember was imprisoned, bound in chains within the dungeon, destined to be sacrificed by Kairlu as part of his ascension ritual.

---

## The Escape

Weeks passed.

Then, in the dead of night, Coryth came.

He freed her.

> “Kairlu will not let this go. You are his path to power, and his failure will be punished.
> 
> Go. Run. Hide. Train.
> 
> Be ready—for him, and for anyone he sends after you.
> 
> I cannot go with you. The mark upon me would allow them to track me… and thus, you.
> 
> Take these. Relics of my past—and enough gold to survive.
> 
> Hide the horns. Hide the tail.
> 
> Until you are far from here.”

That was the last Ember saw of Coryth Bladesinger.
