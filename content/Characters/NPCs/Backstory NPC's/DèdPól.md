# **DèdPól – From Slave to Shade**
DèdPól was not born to power. She was born on the outskirts of [[Westbrook]] , in the farmlands of the Isle of [[Zerith]]—a place where life was predictable, hard, and small. She was never meant to be anything more than a farmer. She refused.
## **The First Escape – The Daelen Wood** 
	At ten years old, she ran. Hungry, desperate, and alone, she fled into the **Daelen Wood**, where she nearly starved before being captured by the wild elves who dwelled there. They did not kill her. They kept her.
##  **The Comfortable Cage** 
	 In the elven settlement: 
	- She was fed
	- Sheltered
	- Given work
	And for a time… she was almost happy. The work was easier.   The days were shorter.  She could wander the woods. But she was never free.
	As years passed: The elves did not age while she clearly did
	She realized the truth: 
	**A pleasant life is still a prison if you cannot leave it.**

## **The Second Escape – Marduk** 
	On a trade convoy to the **White Lotus Academy** in the Khargast Mountains, everything changed. There was a new master. **Marduk.** The elves saw a wizard. DèdPól saw something else: **Power**.
	That night, she went to him. Not as a slave. As someone willing to bargain. He bought her for **five gold**.

## **The Pact**
	At the academy, she learned quickly. But knowledge wasn’t enough. 
	She wanted:    
	- **Power**
	- **To stop aging**
	- **To never be beneath anyone again**
	Marduk revealed the truth: He was no wizard. He was a **Balor Lord**. And he made her an offer:  **60 years of service** In exchange for: **Power and Eternal youth.** She accepted without hesitation.

## **The Lie**
	For fifty years, she served flawlessly.
	She:
		- Maintained the illusion of the academy
	    - Acted as Marduk’s agent in mortal affairs
	    - Grew in power
	She believed she was winning. Freedom and power were within her grasp

 **The Death**
	A cave-in. A simple, meaningless accident. Buried alive. As she died, she felt relief. “At least I’m free.”

## **The Truth of the Pact**
	Her soul rose. And was pulled directly to Marduk. Confused, she asked why. He answered simply: **“You still have ten years.”** 
	Death had not freed her. It had _refined her usefulness_.

## **The Shade**
	In undeath, she became something else: A being of shadow, bound, but unbound from flesh. Able to move between planes.
	Marduk sent her to the **Shadowfell**. There, she became: His spy; His whisper; His unseen hand.  She served the final ten years.
	And when it ended…

## **Freedom (Sort Of)**
	Marduk kept his word. He released her. But by then:  She had no body, No life, and no place in the mortal world.
	So she stayed in the **Shadowfell**.

## **Gloomwrought & Obsession**
	She settled in **Gloomwrought**, in the libraries and forgotten archives.	She searched for one thing: **A way to undo what she had become.**
	Not power. Not dominance. Not even revenge. **Life.**

## **The Discovery**
	In a damaged, ancient journal found in one of Marduk's libraries—written in a language even magic could not fully decipher—she found a clue.
	One phrase remained clear:
	**“The Tetrahedron de Orgone Vontinere”**  (The Tetrahedron of Lifeforce Containment)

## **The Destination**
An artifact. 
A solution. 
A promise.

# **The Plot:**
	DèdPól plans to reassemble the tetrahedron, and use it to restore herself back to life, then seek revenge on those who she feel wronged her in her life.  She views her disciples as weak and expendable.
