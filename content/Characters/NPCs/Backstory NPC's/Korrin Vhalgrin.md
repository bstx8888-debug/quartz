## Basic Information  
- **Race:** Mountain Dwarf  
- **Role:** Lieutenant of the Technomancers  
- **Affiliation (Public):** Technomancers  
- **Affiliation (Secret):** Landiss  
- **Occupation:** Master Fabricator / Logistics Coordinator  
  
---  
  
## Description  
Korrin Vhalgrin is a broad-shouldered dwarf with soot-darkened hands and a perpetually furrowed brow. His beard is neatly braided with small metal clasps—each representing a completed project or successful commission.  
  
He carries himself with quiet confidence, speaking only when necessary, but always with purpose. His presence in the workshop is steadying—methodical, precise, and dependable.  
  
---  
  
## Personality  
- Practical and grounded  
- Values efficiency over elegance  
- Believes strongly in structure and order  
- Loyal—though not always to the right cause  
- Speaks plainly, avoids unnecessary embellishment  
  
---  
  
## Public Role (Technomancers)  
Korrin serves as Drafous’s second-in-command within the Technomancers.  
  
He is responsible for:  
- Scaling production of magical items  
- Training apprentices in practical fabrication  
- Managing supply chains and distribution  
- Ensuring designs are reproducible and stable  
  
While Drafous innovates, Korrin ensures those innovations can reach the masses.  
  
---  
  
## Secret Role (Landiss Agent)  
Korrin is an embedded operative of the [[Landiss]].  
  
He was placed within the Technomancers to:  
- Monitor the spread of independent magical technology  
- Report on individuals gaining autonomy from wizard factions  
- Identify destabilizing innovations  
- Sabotage the guild if it becomes a threat  
  
He has operated undetected for years.  
  
---  
  
## Motivation  
Korrin does not see himself as a traitor.  
  
He believes:  
> Unregulated access to magical tools will destabilize society and lead to chaos.  
  
To him, the Technomancers—while well-intentioned—are dangerous.  
  
Drafous is viewed as:  
- Brilliant  
- Visionary  
- Ultimately reckless  
  
---  
  
## Relationship with Drafous  
Korrin is one of the few individuals Drafous fully trusts.  
  
He has:  
- Helped refine and stabilize many of Drafous’s designs  
- Potentially contributed to the construction of **Prismcaster**  
- Acted as a grounding force for the guild’s rapid expansion  
  
This makes his eventual betrayal deeply personal.  
  
---  
  
## Involvement in Betrayal  
Korrin played a direct role in the events leading to Drafous’s capture.  
  
Possible actions include:  
- Informing the [[Landiss]] of Drafous’s meeting with [[Zanzer Tem]]  
- Altering schedules or communications to isolate Drafous  
- Ensuring no support accompanied him  
  
Drafous does not yet know this.  
  
---  
  
## Notes  
- Korrin genuinely believes he is preventing a greater disaster  
- His loyalty is to order, not individuals  
- May struggle internally if confronted, but will not easily yield  
- Could serve as a future antagonist or tragic foil
