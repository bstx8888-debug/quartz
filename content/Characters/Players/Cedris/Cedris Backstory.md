Cedris was born in shadow.  More specifically, she was born on the lowest level of the City of [[Molrenai - Forge city of the Shadowfell|Molrenai]] - Forge City of the [[Shadowfell]].  

Cedris was resigned from an early age to escape the life of a miner. Poverty stricken, barely more than an indentured servant, that was a life clearly meant for someone else, but without means to get enough money together to leave, prospects were grim.  When a Shaadee by the name of  [[DèdPól]] came through the lowest level looking for assistants, promising a life beyond [[Molrenai - Forge city of the Shadowfell|Morlrenai]], Cedris had no hesitation.  Personal danger was no deterrent, as mines collapsed all the time killing those within.  [[DèdPól]] was salvation. With her help, Cedris and a handful of other Shadar-Kai, left the city together, setting sail for the merchant city of [[Gloomwrought]].

![[Cedris and DèdPól Together.png|500]]
During their journey, Cedris, and the other [[Daughters of DèdPól]] as they had taken to calling themselves, swore a pact allegiance to their new Master.  In exchange for their freedom, better lives, and a portion of  [[DèdPól]]’s arcane power, they would swear loyalty, and perform tasks for her unquestioningly.  Upon arrival in  [[Gloomwrought]], their first tasks were assigned.  Cedris, having a strong affinity for moving unseen, was sent to the material plane to find the locations (and if possible retrieve) 4 great artefacts capable of channeling immense power.  Each would be a great gem inset in a metal triangle.

The book describing the pieces was old and tattered, and [[DèdPól]] rarely let it leave her sight.  But she did let Cedris see it enough to memorize the triangle pieces so she might identify them.

·         A large white pearl, set in gold
·         A large black Pearl, set in silver
·         A large pink pearl, set in bronze
·         A large grey pearl, set in copper

While studying the descriptions and diagrams, Cedris did get a peek at the next page, which showed the triangles forming a pyramid, and a great light coming from within.

[[DèdPól]] knew there would be little knowledge to go on, and once in  [[Gloomwrought]] sought out a soothsayer to guide Cedris.  The guidance was small, but a start.   Find [[Noc]] of the [[Many Stars]], and accompany him. It would lead her to at least the first part. Finding the rest would be on her. She set forth through the portal, and landed in the [[Zerith|Mortal World]].

Never before had the sun been so bright. Never before had she experienced breath without the oppressive melancholy of the Shadowfell.  Never before had she had the freedom to go where she chose.  And she both loved and hated it.

Arriving in the town of [[Corvus]], she quickly learned that the Coven of the Many Stars did not have a headquarters, but she was able to connect with some members of the order, who eventually led her to connect with [[Noc]] who happened to be in the city, on his way to the [[Damarian]] academy to investigate news of an attack. Suggesting safety in numbers, she managed to convince him to allow her to accompany him.
