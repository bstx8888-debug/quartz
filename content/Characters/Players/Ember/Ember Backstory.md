
Hidden away in the [[Khargast Mountains]], a secret school of magic existed.  They called themselves the [[White Lotus]] Academy.  The school taught many things, but above all, secrecy.  While any with aptitude would be welcome, details were always vague when the scouts would recruit new members.

Being so welcoming to any with magical powers had its drawbacks.  Soon, a trend started of bargaining for power with demons and devils, the path of the Warlock.  Learning too late the folly of such ways when done in large groups, the school was eventually thrown into chaos, and a fiery and bloody civil war broke out within the academy, ending in the demise of the headmaster, and most of the staff.  The new reign of warlocks, calling themselves the Pyracanth, continued the school, but as a recruiting home to bring in servants and slaves for their Balor Lord, [[Marduk]].  Soon many a Tiefling were born, and trained in the ways of the academy.  When they came of age, at 21, they would accept his demonic gifts, growing into a warlock army at his disposal.

![[ember.png|450]]

One such Tiefling, Ember, never felt at peace or calm in the lifestyle she was born to.  Seeing this as she grew, one professor from the old ways of the White Lotus, took her under his wing.  As a Master in the ways of the Rose King, the Eladrin warrior, Professor [[Coryth Bladesinger]], taught her, and a select few students the ancient blend of the eldritch knight, combining the martial dance of the sword with Magical enhancements.  “Ages ago, there were legions of us knights” he’d say often, before regaling his select few with tales of times gone by.

Ember learned all she could from him, a natural with both blade and magic.  She watched as the older students of his would often give up his teachings and take [[Marduk|Marduk's]] boon. [[Coryth Bladesinger]] would grow sadder with each student that turned their back on his teachings, and she vowed to not do the same.  One of her older peers, an ambitious warlock named [[Kairlu]], saw her hesitation as the day of her 19th birthday loomed on the horizon.  He did not share her conviction and was all too ready to accept the power promised by [[Marduk]].  He betrayed Ember, knowing full well that the sentence would be her death, but also assuring his proven loyalty to his new master, which would include a higher rank in the order.

Held in chains in the dungeon, Ember was to be slain by [[Kairlu]] as part of his ceremony when the time came.  Weeks passed, until [[Coryth Bladesinger]] visited her, and under in the dead of night, aided in her escape.

“[[Kairlu]] will not let this go, as you are his path to power, and his failure will surely be punished.  Go.  Run.  Hide.  Train.  Be ready for him, and anyone he sends for you. I have to stay, as the mark upon me would allow them to track me, and thus you.  Take these items, relics of my past, and this bit of gold to get you through.  Hide the horns and tail until you are far from here.”

That was nearly two years ago.  Ember has hitched along caravan to caravan, always keeping a low profile, using minor magiks and special clothes and head dresses to remain undetected as she made her way west, as far away from the [[Khargast Mountains]] and her brother as possible.  Trying to find her own way in the world, she has tapped both into her disciplined teachings and her demonic heritage.

Her 21st birthday is coming soon, and Ember has been plagued with nightmares of the demons of hell hunting her.  [[Kairlu]] is coming.
