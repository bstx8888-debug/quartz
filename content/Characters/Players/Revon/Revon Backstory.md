Revon Hoxley, born Revon Elenarion Falte, is an Eladrin wizard apprentice of the [[Feywild]].

Revon was born to nobility as a member of the Glacial Stone, an Eladrin Winter Court. From a young age he had an aptitude for magic, but couldn't seem to control its manifestation. He was inducted into the Winterwood Institute of higher magic, and placed in the care of a family friend, [[Ruith Fawenys]].  [[Ruith Fawenys|Ruith]], originally from the one of the Spring Courts, was one of the masters of the Winterwood Institute, and took on a mentorship of the young pupil. The hope was to help Revon to control his chaos magic.  Had it been discovered, he would have been magically bound, and exiled to be a field worker, as chaos magic is frowned upon, in favor of more refined practice. The danger of the chaos setting fire to forests and causing towers to fall was not worth the risk.

It was at the Seasonal Change Celebration that most Eladrin Nobility of each court had gathered at the Spring capital to welcome the next cycle change.  Revon had remained in his home in the winter realm, practicing magical artisanship which had been his most recent lesson. Eventually he grew bored of it though, and began small experiments tapping into his chaos magic out of simple fascination, since no one else was around.

Without warning, a terrible, sinister snowstorm swept in from the Winter Realm. This blizzard heralded great destruction, killing several nobles before it was stopped.  The [[Feywild Blizzard|dark storm]] had come from the Winter border, sweeping across the land.  Following it's trail back to the border, it was clear a hole had opened up between the realms of spring and winter, though it had now closed itself. While no one from the Winter Court would admit knowing how such a thing could have happened, scouts from the other courts were already setting out to find who or what may have caused it. [[Ruith Fawenys|Ruith]], fearing Revon would be blamed, sent word to Revon to flee. Revon gathered what he could and fled the Institute. During his travels, Revon fell through a portal where [[Zerith]] and the [[Feywild]] were especially close. This coupled with being beaten and kidnapped by orcs led to something of an apparent amnesia. Whether he really remembers or not, Revon took on a new surname simply meaning Sorcerer and disgraced by his home and his familial legacy, desired redemption and the truth of what happened that day, for had it truly been his fault or was something else at work?
