Faelyn Cloudshaper was born in [[Brolin]] to a humble cobbler.  While a skilled artisan, her family had no prestige to speak of.  The Halflings of [[Brolin]] were a small tribe, but meshed well with the humans and elves of the city.  The nearby Enclave of Druids, the [[Tarin]], kept the city safe, from the threats of the [[Moonglade Forest]], and the port meant there was always some bustle of merchants coming and going, which kept her family comfortable.  As a young Halfling, Faelyn was free to roam the city, spending a lot of time walking along the beach, and playing in the [[Moonglade Forest]] which ran up to the edge of the city.

She always enjoyed playing out away from the edges of the city, while her fellow Halfling friends much preferred a city life, with good food and quiet gossip to pass their time.  This meant she often explored on her own, finding little nooks and crannies where the forest met the beach, sure that she’d find buried pirate treasure, that surely no civilized person had ever stepped foot into that particular meadow before.

One day, while exploring on her own, around age eight, she came across a wounded jaguar cub, with a clearly broken hind leg. Having just healed from her own broken leg (climbing a tree while it was covered in wet moss may not have been her smartest move that day), she could see the animal’s pain.  Without thought for her safety, she rushed to its side, crushing up some mellow-leaf to help with pain and grabbing some sticks and vines to make a splint.  As she approached, the cat, who was easily as big as her, prepared to strike, but she was undeterred.  Holding her hands out and speaking softly, she managed to get close, giving it some of the leaf to munch on, and gently applying some to the leg where it was bleeding.  Gently, pausing whenever the cub would start to react, she set the leg in the splint.  It was then she realized she was not alone in the clearing.  A much larger jaguar, likely the cubs mother, had been approaching, and was now standing over the Halfling.  While she knew she was in danger, she grit her teeth, and focused on setting the cub’s leg.  If she was going to be decapitated by a Jaguar, she was at least going to finish helping the baby first.  She set the leg and tied the splint.  She slowly rose, removing herself from between the mother and cub.  The mother bore down on Faelyn, but as the Halfling slowly backed away, the mother chose to tend to her cub which was now standing thanks to the splint.

Breathing a sigh of relief, Faelyn decided to make her way home, when she was intercepted by Zelara, one of the Druids of the Tarin.  Commending her bravery and compassion, while chastising her stupidity, Zelara informed her that she was clearly destined to be a druid.  After a brief discussion with her parents, Faelyn was inducted into the first circle of druids.  Faelyn loved her work, and as her powers grew, she rose through the ranks of the druid circles.

![[Riendaith.png||350]]

Of her many adventures protecting the Moonglade and it’s coast, perhaps her fondest was when she met [[Riendaith]].  One summer day, on patrol (or excursion as she preferred to call it) along the coast several days from Brolin, she was suddenly set upon by a summer storm.  Seeking shelter in a large coastal cave, she came across multiple wrecked pirate ships.  Clearly washed into the cave by the incoming tide, the ships were torn asunder on the rocks within.  Giddy with glee at her find, she practically flew down to the ships to start exploring and look for treasure (a pastime she had yet to truly give up.)

On the first two ships she found nothing, no signs of treasure or bodies, much to her disappointment.  In the hull of the third ship though she found a pile of gold and gems that boggled her young mind. What she didn’t expect was sitting atop the treasure was a young bronze dragon. Aware of her presence, it sat, coiled and ready to attack.   Always… oblivious to her danger, Faelyn immediately started talking to the dragon, gushing over the amazing pile of coins, asking if it was all pirate treasure, how much was there, did it all come from the crashed ships, is there more under the water, oh yeah, what’s your name, I’m Faelyn by the way, but anyway do you like gold or gems more?

The dragon, amused at not only the lack of fear, but the sheer appreciation for their fledgling horde, immediately took a liking to the little ball of energy that had stumbled into their lair. Speaking in a booming voice that hushed the Halfling (if only for a moment) they said “I am [[Riendaith]]” (pronounced Ree-En-Dayth) “and welcome to my home “little wolf”.  Please, come and sit with me and I will tell you of my hoard.” As she approached, and started to sit, the ground beneath her shifted.  The whole ship started to move and turn, as the swell of the tide broke loose the ships, and they started to roll, then sink. [[Riendaith]] was struck by a large beam, falling unconscious.  Faelyn quickly grabbed a rope, tied it around the dragon, then changed into a bird, and flew out of the ship holding the other end, and tied it to a stalagmite. As the ship rolled and sank, the rope held and fell away from the dragon.  She slowly reeled them onto the dry floor out of the water, and lit a fire with some driftwood in the cave.  While the dragon slept and recuperated, she set to work.

By the time [[Riendaith]] woke, a couple hours later, he was surrounded by a pile of gold and gems, lying next to a fire, and a soggy sleeping Halfling. Waking her, and asking what had happened, she told him of pulling him from the ship, and that she had heard that dragons healed best when laying on their hoard, so she swam down to the sinking ship and recovered as much as she could.

From that day, the two became fast friends.  [[Riendaith]] blessed Faelyn with a dragon-mark, bonding them, and showing her as friend to all dragons. Together they would attack and raid pirate ships that dared to raid the coast near the Moonglade. Riendaith would blast the ships with their lightning breath, while Faelyn would use the powers of nature, and her beastly form to dispatch the pirates. They’d return what they could to those who lost it, but also kept the rest to grow [[Riendaith|Riendaith's]] hoard, as well as give some special pieces to Faelyn to help her fight the pirates.

Most recently, she was sent on a ship to [[Porin]], to collect sample from the expansive desert, and bring them back to Brolin to monitor the progress of the desert sands.  On the trip back, her ship ran afoul of a whirlpool, and was drug beneath the waves.  Having wild-shaped as the ship went down, she managed to stay conscious as her ship and crew were drug beneath the waves.  Her and a handful of crew were drug off the deck up the ship, being pulled through some caves until caught in a net.  She climbed from the water, and managed to hide as the net pulled her crewmates from the water.  Men came, woke them up, and fed them a moss.  She watched as they described the fish they were eating and the blankets they were being given, and her crewmates accepted this as fact.  Following them in the form of a small beast, she was led into a large city.  For the last 6 weeks she has stayed hidden, catching fish from the entrance, and trying to figure out a way to escape. Ships would crash and new people welcomed into the city, but they would all be subdued before even entering the city.  The last ship was a week-ish ago, and the crew that made it stood out from the regular sailors.  Suddenly though, the hum of the cavern shifted, and all of the citizens suddenly started to get aggressive, and make their way to the palace.  Rushing, she saw the latest crew was making their way into the palace doors, and she bolted in, sneaking in behind them as the doors shut.
