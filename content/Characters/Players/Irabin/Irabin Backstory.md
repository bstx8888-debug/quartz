Irabin Sylvar Trismaris was born in the city of Lily, in the [[Feywild Basemap.png|Spring Realm of the Fey]].  Like all those of the Pink Court reaching adulthood at 17, Irabin set out on a pilgrimage to traverse the Feywild seasons.  Unlike other nobles of his station though, he set out alone, without a Caravan or guards to keep him safe as he traversed across the lands.  Straying from the road, he headed eastward. Towards the base of the Snap-Dragon Mountains, then east to Tulip.  Crossing the plains of spring he was met with weather, and the occasional wild animal, but the journey was easy enough. 

Once passing into summer though, the trek got harder.  He was unprepared for the sweltering heat combined with the high humidity.  The naturally sweet scent of the magic infused lad became a suffocating perfume. Sweating through pores he didn’t know Eladrin had, he pressed east, until reaching the great road. The road would take him north to Blossom Tower or south and east to Mediodia, to rest and resupply, but for now his water supply was dwindling.  He was going through it faster than expected due to the extreme weather. He wondered if he should have taken a caravan, as others of his station normally would. 

Examining his map, he was likely 2 months from the tower, but there was a river to the east.  Branching from the road once more, he traveled towards water.  He passed marshlands and small stagnant lakes, whose water he was hesitant to drink from while his supplies lasted.  Passing through he saw all manner of creatures and birds of all sorts of colors.  Reaching the river, the water was clear and cool, and Irabin camped by the river overnight, bathing and refilling his supplies. It was there he died.

Waking in the night to a searing pain in his lower half, some creature of the dark tore into his lower half.  As he laid there bleeding and fading from consciousness, he beseeched the spirits around him to aid him.  Suddenly a bright mist formed from the water, sweeping up, and knocking the shadowing figure away into the water.  As unconsciousness took him, the misty creature loomed over the dying Eladrin.

Irabin awoke with a start. He was in pain on his lower half, but examining himself, there were simply scars from where something had torn into him. As he took in his surroundings, he noted the brightness of the Feywild was dimmed, and he could see something on the edges of his sight.  Still by the river, something called to him, and he answered. Coalescing before him stood one of the mighty pink birds he saw in the marshes.  The two stared at one another, bowed, and knew they were now linked.  The bird’s name was… Jerry.

Irabin, now restocked on water, continued north along the river, allowing the natural coolness of the rushing water to keep the summer heat at bay.  Jerry walked with him, and while not much of a conversationalist, was an excellent listener.  Irabin became more and more in tune with Jerry as they traveled, and through him became more attuned with the spirit realm itself.  He felt the ebb and flow of life, through Jerry as his medium.  He also felt himself attune to the land, to the breath of summer itself. By the time he reached the Blossom Tower, he was unrecognizable as a Eladrin of Spring, his blonde locks shifting to a shimmering bronze, his skin deeply tanned.  Resting for a mere day, he immediately set out, ever eastward again, reaching the barrier to the lands of Autumn. 

Passing through, he felt the energy of the season shift, far more pronounced than the barrier to spring.  His new attunement mean he felt the peak of growth turn to a decline, like going from high noon to evening sunset.  While still teeming with life and death, the change between the two was subtler.  As the land and energy changed, so too did Irabin.  His hair shifting from the bronze to copper, matching the falling leaves.  His tan softening, Irabin noticed that Jerry too seemed to change. His bright ethereal form took on a tan hue, matching with Irabin’s changes. He continues his journey, attuning to the land of autumn as he had to summer. Reaching Verais, then taking the road to Koran-Tir, he was welcomes, though got many a strange look.  Passing through the portal from Koran-Tir to the Glass Tower of Winter, he set out on his final stage of the journey.  His hair silver like the snow, his features pale.  Jerry too became a start spiritual white.  Irabin continued his pilgrimage east, eventually reaching his home spring lands, re-attuned to his home, looked like himself once more. Jerry took on a hint of a greenish blue, but was still definitely Jerry.

Welcomed back to the spring court, he spoke of his journey with reverence, though was always hesitant to speak of his near death experience, and of Jerry.  While there was a veneration for nature, the respectable Eladrin courts focus on the study and pursuit of the arcane.  As such, Irabin carried on, maintaining the trappings of court, though would often find excuses to travel out to the other lands, keeping his attunement with nature thriving and active.

On one such trip, 200 years since his initial pilgrimage, Irabin travelled to the Spring Capitol of Snap Dragon for its annual welcoming of Spring.  With the three moons at their peak, magic in the Feywild flows freely, naturally intoxicating the fey of the realm.  During the three-day revelry, a huge [[Feywild Blizzard]] rose over the mountains to the west, descending on the castle.  The flash freeze took many lives of those who could not find cover in time.

Members of courts from all across the Feywild were present, and now they all sought justice.  Following the trail of the blizzard west, they passed the City of Lily, which had been in the direct path.  While there were survivors, almost all of the court of pink had been wiped out.

Once they caravan reached the winter border, they could tell that the barrier between the realms had opened, and the great storm come through.  While the barrier had healed, I was still weak.  Several of the fey set to work on fully repairing it, while others set out to find evidence of who was responsible. The consensus was that it must be a winter Burlani, though any known to be powerful enough were at the Spring Court when the event happened.  Passage was granted to the winter realm though, and several investigators, Irabin included, journeyed to the Glass Tower to try to gather more information.

On the journey, Irabin’s special attunement allowed him to follow a path no one else did.  Someone recently had travelled a portal to the Mortal plane.  Examining the site, some shift of the ether happened, the portal opened, and Irabin too was sucked through.  Unfortunately, the portals tend to be one way.  He was in a large plain, next to a solitary mountain.  He headed northward, Jerry by his side eventually arriving in the port town of Melanni. 

While adept with primal forces of nature, it soon became clear that Zerith ran on arcane magic, much like the fey wild.  There was one “wizard cabal” though, The Tarin, which professed a respect for the land, and claimed to gain its strength from it.  Sensing a possibly kindred spirit, Irabin set out to join this group, which was comprised of several druids, but also other Shamans. Once joined in the order, they provided him supplies, but more importantly, information.  Eladrin are few.  One of the Tarin druids said that while flying across the land, they saw an Eladrin that looked similar to Irabin, with silver hair, to the south, near the lone mountain of Khalen’s peak.  While the druid did not see which way the other Eladrin went, he recommended Irabin travel to the city of Brolin.  The main Tarin enclave was based out of the Moonglade, and had contacts that could help locate this lone Eladrin, if he got there soon enough.  Setting out by boat, he had hoped to arrive in Brolin soon, until his ship was lost at sea.

Waking up, he was surrounded by the courts of the Eladrin.  Food was plentiful, wine flowed freely, and he lived in a state of revelry, despite the nagging feeling that something was missing. Somewhere at the edge of his vision was a bird, but he could never quite see it. As he was on his way to join the ball, he woke from the bad dream.
