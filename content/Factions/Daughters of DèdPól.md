# The Daughters of DèdPól  

![[Daughters of DèdPól.png]]
  
A coterie of twelve Shadar-kai warlocks bound to [[DèdPól]], drawn from the poorest depths of a Shadowfell city with promises of power and wealth.  
  
Led by [[Cedris]], the most powerful and refined of the daughters.  
  
---  
  
## Core Dynamic  
  
- [[Cedris]] → Apex figure, embodiment of success  
- True Believers → Nyxara, Thalra  
- Pragmatists → Sereth, Virelle  
- Enforcers → Kaelith, Eryndra  
- Unsettling Elements → Morvane, Drelka  
- Tension & Suspicion → Vaelith, Sylthrae  
- Moral Fracture → Orlisse  
  
---  
  
## Members  
  
### Vaelith  
- Cold, calculating, speaks only when necessary  
- Treats everything like a long game  
- Quietly resents [[Cedris]]    
### Nyxara  
- Fanatically devoted to [[DèdPól]]  
- Sees her patron as salvation  
- Willingly embraces suffering in service  
### Sereth  
- Former street thief  
- Opportunistic and pragmatic  
- Loyal because this is the best life she’s ever had  
### Morvane  
- Soft-spoken and unsettling  
- Fascinated by death and souls  
- Lingers around corpses longer than necessary  
### Thalra  
- Proud, aristocratic demeanor  
- Fully embraces newfound superiority  
- Looks down on mortals and weaker sisters  
### Eryndra  
- Bitter and volatile  
- Uses magic aggressively  
- Feels the world owes her  
### Virelle  
- Charismatic and manipulative  
- Handles negotiation and recruitment  
- Smiles often, rarely means it 
### Kaelith  
- Silent enforcer  
- Speaks rarely, acts decisively  
- Called when something must end  
### Drelka  
- Dark, playful humor  
- Treats grim situations lightly  
- Laughs at inappropriate moments  
### Sylthrae  
- Deeply paranoid  
- Constantly watching for betrayal  
- Extremely difficult to deceive  
### Orlisse  
- Most “human” of the group  
- Remembers her former life vividly  
- Occasionally shows mercy  
## Notes  
All members of The Daughters of DèdPól originate from the lowest tier of Molrenai.  
- Born into poverty and labor  
- Exposed early to death and suffering  
- Recruited by [[DèdPól]]    
### The Pact  
  DèdPól offers:  
- Power  
- Escape from the caste system  
- Wealth and status  
In return:  
- Absolute loyalty  
- Their souls and service
