
![[Technomancers.png|800]]

Totaling roughly 60 members strong, the Technomancers were formed with the sole purpose of bringing permanent magical support to the masses at an affordable price.  Rather than pay a wizard a repeated fee, buying a simple magic items that can perform the same task and using it freely would be helpful to the poorer resident's of Zerith, as well as turn a profit for those who provide those items.
The top 10 members of the guild are referred to as the Tenfold Circuit.  They are in charge, running guild operations, setting up trade and sale contracts, and recruiting and training the junior members.  The other 50 members are craftsmen, paid well for creating the supply of magic items used to aid the populous of Zerith.
# ⚙️ The Tenfold Circuit

## 1. [[Drafous]]
- **Race:** Shardmind
- **Role:** Arcane Innovator
- **Demeanor:** Idealistic, thoughtful, tactical
Drafous was the founder of the Technomancer's, with immense help from [[Korrin Vhalgrin]]
## 2. **[[Korrin Vhalgrin]]**
- **Race:** Dwarf
- **Role:** Lieutenant of the Technomancers
- **Demeanor:** Practical and grounded , values efficiency over elegance, speaks plainly
Korrin joined Drafous when founding the technomancers, hoping to make the world a better place.  While Drafous may be the dreamer, Korrin is the person to execute those dreams.
## 3. **Elira Vance**
- **Race:** Human
- **Role:** Arcane Systems Designer
- **Demeanor:** Curious, fast-talking, relentlessly innovative
Elira is the mind that pushes boundaries. She’s always trying to improve designs—sometimes _too much_. Where Drafous is precise, she’s experimental. Often responsible for prototype instability… and breakthroughs.
## 4. **Brannik Gearfall**
- **Race:** Rock Gnome
- **Role:** Miniaturization Specialist
- **Demeanor:** Energetic, obsessive, easily distracted
Brannik makes everything _smaller, cheaper, and more efficient_. Talks to devices like they’re alive. Frequently forgets to sleep.
## 5. **Sera Nighthollow**
- **Race:** Half-Elf
- **Role:** Field Tester / Retrieval Specialist
- **Demeanor:** Cool, pragmatic, quietly sarcastic
Sera tests prototypes in real-world conditions. If something survives her, it’s ready. She’s also the one who retrieves “lost” items… or people.
## 6. **Tholm Granitehand**
- **Race:** Hill Dwarf
- **Role:** Materials Engineer
- **Demeanor:** Patient, traditional, stubborn
Tholm ensures everything is built to last. Distrusts “flashy” magic and insists on durability over elegance. Has butted heads with Elira more than once.
## 7. **Irielle Sunthread**
- **Race:** High Elf
- **Role:** Enchantment Theory Specialist
- **Demeanor:** Calm, academic, slightly aloof
Irielle bridges classical wizardry and Drafous’s applied philosophy. She ensures enchantments are stable and scalable. Quietly fascinated by Drafous’s methods.
## 8. **Marek Doss**
- **Race:** Human
- **Role:** Distribution & Client Relations
- **Demeanor:** Charismatic, diplomatic, street-smart
Marek handles the people side—negotiations, delivery networks, and keeping the guild from stepping on powerful toes. Knows how to talk to both nobles and commoners.
## 9. **Zix “Copperwire”**
- **Race:** Goblin
- **Role:** Energy Conduit Specialist
- **Demeanor:** Chaotic, brilliant, slightly unhinged
Zix works with volatile energy systems—power sources, conduits, and charge mechanisms. Frequently singed. Absolutely indispensable.
## 10. **Valen Torr**
- **Race:** Tiefling
- **Role:** Experimental Weapons Designer
- **Demeanor:** Intense, perfectionist, competitive
Valen focuses on weaponized applications (with Drafous’s oversight). Deeply respects Drafous, but constantly tries to outdo him. Might be the one most obsessed with **Prismcaster**.
# 🧩 Group Dynamic Snapshot

- **Drafous** → Visionary leader
- **Korrin** → Grounded executor
- **Elira / Brannik / Zix** → Innovation chaos trio
- **Tholm / Irielle** → Stability and theory balance
- **Sera / Marek** → Real-world interface
- **Valen** → Defensive realist
