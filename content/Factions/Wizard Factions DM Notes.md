Landiss:  Our actual antagonists of the story.  They are "neutral" wizards who take it upon themselves to police other cabals, in the name of protecting people or for what they deem for the good of magic.  They have a wide variety of magics at their disposal.  The top members actually covet magic and plan to eliminate all other factions so they can control magic, and the world.  Given their general nature, several wizards have infiltrated other factions to help in the upcoming war.

Damarian (Duh mar e an): Enchanters and illusionists.  Often recruited as spies, or holdin positions of power within the courts of rulers - the "evil visier" trope.

Tarin (Tar in): who proclaim stewardship and protection over the land itelf (from which they gain their name).  Druids, Shamans, wizards, who can bring fair weather, or rain for crops.  Possibly the most wholesome of all the groups.

Many Stars: diviners, oracles, and rital casters.  Shamans can also be found here, looking past the veil to commune with those lost.  They the heavens for guidance and prophecy, acting as oracles for anyone in need or who has the coin.

Evoden - Powerful Evokers, often recruited for war.  They can turn the tide of battle with their powerful spells.

Necrathillian - Powerful necromancers, founded by a dustman from Digil.  His (leader) goal is still to find the true peace and enlightenment of death, but brings the knowledge of his faction.

White Lotus / Pyracanth: This academy, hidden in the mountains near Fort Durkon, has fallen under the sway of Marduk, a powerful Balor Lord.  Tieflings and warlocks abound.

Rose King: There are some disciples of the way of the Rose King, that have traversed from the Feywild into Zerith.  They have ended up at some of the greater accademies, trying to share the teaching with students there, and trying to push back the impending darkness.

 Technomancers:  and artisan guild intent on bringing magic to the people through items that can help them in their daily life.
