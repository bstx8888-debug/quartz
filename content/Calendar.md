Months are usually abbreviated to 2 Letters, VA, VB, VC, etc.

Each season is 3 months long, with spring starting on the new year, VA1

Solstace’s fall on VK15 and VE15.  Equinox’s fall on VH15 and VB15

Weeks in Zerith last 10 days, and are split into 3 weeks per month.

The days of the week are

QenArd, QenBev, QenCad, QenDin, QenErz, QenFon, QenGud, QenHal, QenIrx, QenJun.

Zerith has 3 moons. 

The brightest is Ard, and has a month long lunar cycle, with the full moon on the first of each month.

The second, largest moon is Bev.  It has a 90 day lunar cycle, with it’s first full moon on the first day of the season.

The third and smallest moon is Cad.  It has a 10 day lunar cycle, with it’s first full moon of each month falling on the first with Ard.

There are triple full moons on the first of each season, and Triple new moons on the equinox’s and solstices.
