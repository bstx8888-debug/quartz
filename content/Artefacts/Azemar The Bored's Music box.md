# Focus
![[Musicbox Implement.png|500]]
The focus piece of the music box is a small gear driven music box, and counts as a +5 level 22 focus, usable by any arcane class. Driven by magic, the gear can play forever, withtout winding.

The problem is the latch that engages the gear to the music box is jammed, so the music box cannot turn off. Wielding the music box in this state negates stealth of the wearer, though while stowed away in a sealed container, it simply confers a -2 penalty to the bearer.

Azemar never turned it off, so he did not notice. This can be repaired via an easy skill challenge (3 successes before 2 failures) and must bve performed by at least 2 players. The appropriate skills are perception, thievery, and arcana. (one of each must be performed) DC is 18 for all checks. Success fixes the broken latch, failure permanently breaks it, meaning the music box will never stop playing unless detroyed.

Until fixed, the music box cannot recharge it's encounter or daily ability.

At-Will power - Change the music to a new tune of your choosing.

Encounter Power - Confer combat advantage to you and your allies until the start of your next turn

Daily Power - Allow a power with a sustain minor to be sustained until the end of the encounter, or until the music box is turned off. This can be dispelled at will by the caster as a free action (ending the effect immediately)

# Amiplifier
![[Amplifier.png|500]]

While the music box is inserted into the amplifier and playing, any rituals performed in it's vicinity (within 10 squares) are granted a +5 to their associated skill check.

Three times per day, any bardic ritual may be performed without paying the component cost.
