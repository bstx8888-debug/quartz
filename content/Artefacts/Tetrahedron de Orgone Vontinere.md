# History
	The Tetrahedron de Orgone Vontinere is a godly artefact.  Once assembled, it can capture and contain souls.  It was designed to contain the power of a primordial, using four immense pearls formed by cosmic oysters, attuned to each element.  During the deific war between the gods and the primordials, it was forged to store the soul of a primordial, for interrogation and questioning. During the war, the tetrahedron was successfully used to capture more than one primordial, but when the tetrahedron was opened, the resulting explosion killed the god holding it, leaving it's corpse to float in the astral sea.  Several of it's pieces flew across the astral see to the mortal worlds, their natural need to combine keeping them close.  They landed on various iterations of Zerith, burying themselves within the land.  
	Single pieces have been found, and worshipped, but the natural draw of souls by the artefact causes those who possess or worship the pieces to have their soul's slowly drained, eventually killing off those who covet them, but don't know their true purpose.

# Description
	The four triangles of the tetrahedron are:
	·        A large white pearl, set in gold - Attuned to Air
	·        A large black Pearl, set in silver - Attuned to Earth
	·        A large pink pearl, set in bronze - Attuned to Fire
	·        A large grey pearl, set in copper - Attuned to Water

# The Journal
While searching for a cure for undeath,[[DèdPól]] found a journal in an ancient library while in her service to [[Marduk]]. The journal is in a language that predates civilization, and is a combination of Supernal and Primordial.  As such, it is unable to be translated via Comprehend Language.  The journal is tattered, and half burned, but it does describe the pieces of the tetrahedron, and even has some accurate sketches, as well as the description of the ritual to reassemble the tetrahedron.  The reassembly ritual, as well as the other rituals that can be performed using the tetrahedron, are inscribed on the edges of the triangles.

## Journal Entry for Air
### ### Air Aspect — Aurum Spiralis

#### #### Physical Description

The triangular segment attributed to the aerial principle is wrought of bright gold, refined to a near luminous sheen, and shaped with precise equilateral symmetry consistent with the greater tetrahedral construct. At its center rests a white pearl of notable size and clarity, exhibiting a soft internal luster reminiscent of diffused light through mist. The surface is intricately inlaid with diamonds, each cut and placed to form flowing, curvilinear patterns that evoke motion, current, and unseen force. The craftsmanship suggests not merely decorative intent, but a deliberate attempt to depict movement within stillness, as though the piece itself resists remaining static.

---

#### #### Thematic Observations

The aerial aspect appears to govern that which is unbound and in motion, representing not substance but transition. Where other elements assert presence, air implies passage, distance, and the unseen connection between states. It is associated with breath, whisper, and the imperceptible force that carries both sound and spirit. Repeated observation suggests that this piece resonates most strongly when in proximity to dying subjects, particularly at the moment when respiration falters and ceases, indicating a correlation between air and the departure of animating force.

---

#### #### Role in Soul Manipulation

Among the four components, the aerial segment demonstrates the most pronounced influence over the displacement and direction of the soul. It neither anchors nor preserves, but instead facilitates movement—drawing the anima outward, suspending it, or directing it along unseen paths. In controlled conditions, it has been observed to assist in the extraction of a soul from a failing body with notable efficiency, though retention without the cooperation of the remaining elements proves unstable. The aerial principle therefore appears essential in any operation involving the transition of a soul between states, particularly where relocation or reanimation is intended.

---

#### #### Magical Philosophy

It is proposed that the soul is not a fixed entity, but a current—an unseen motion that exists only insofar as it is in passage between states of being. Air, in this context, serves as both medium and conduit, the invisible force through which the anima travels. At the moment of death, it is the first to depart and the least inclined to return, suggesting that the separation of soul and body is not an act of severance, but of release into motion. Thus, mastery of the aerial principle is not the control of the soul itself, but of the pathways through which it flows. Without such control, the soul disperses beyond reach; with it, the practitioner may guide that motion, though never truly arrest it.
## Journal Entry for Earth
### Earth Aspect — Argentum Radix

#### Physical Description

The triangular segment aligned with the terrestrial principle is formed of polished silver, its surface bearing a subdued, reflective quality that favors depth over brilliance. At its center is set a large black pearl, notable not for luster but for its absorption of light, appearing almost void-like when observed directly. The face of the triangle is inlaid with emeralds, shaped into branching, leaf-like patterns that spread outward from the central pearl. These inlays suggest growth arrested in permanence, as though the natural world has been captured and fixed within the metal itself. The structure conveys weight and stability, with an impression of permanence absent in the other components.

---

#### #### Thematic Observations

The terrestrial aspect governs solidity, form, and the imposition of structure upon that which would otherwise disperse. It is associated with bone, root, and foundation—the elements that persist after motion ceases. Unlike the aerial component, which suggests transition, earth represents conclusion and containment. It has demonstrated a measurable resonance with remains, particularly skeletal structures, indicating a strong affinity for that which endures beyond decay. Prolonged exposure appears to suppress erratic movement in bound entities, reinforcing stillness and cohesion.

---

#### #### Role in Soul Manipulation

The function of the terrestrial segment is that of anchoring. Where the aerial principle facilitates movement, earth arrests it, fixing the soul within a defined structure. In experimental applications, it has proven essential in binding anima to physical remains, particularly in cases where structural integrity is limited to bone. Without the stabilizing influence of this component, reanimated subjects exhibit instability, often resulting in collapse or dispersal of animating force. The terrestrial aspect does not summon or direct the soul, but ensures that once placed, it remains.

---

#### #### Magical Philosophy

If the soul is a current, then earth is the vessel into which it is forced. It is not concerned with the origin or destination of the anima, but with its confinement within form. The persistence of bone beyond the decay of flesh suggests that structure is the final argument against dissolution. Thus, mastery of the terrestrial principle lies in the enforcement of boundaries, compelling the soul to inhabit and remain within matter. This is not a natural state, but an imposed one, requiring continual resistance against the inherent tendency of the soul to move beyond constraint.

## Journal Entry for Fire
### Fire Aspect — Aes Ignis

#### Physical Description

The triangular segment corresponding to the ignic principle is cast in brass, its surface bearing a warm, burnished tone that suggests both heat and volatility. At its center is embedded a pink pearl, whose coloration evokes embers rather than flame, glowing subtly as though lit from within. The face is inlaid with rubies, arranged in sharp, angular formations that resemble tongues of fire or fracturing lines of heat. The overall composition conveys tension and latent energy, as if the piece itself exists in a state of restrained combustion.

---

#### Thematic Observations

The ignic aspect governs transformation, intensity, and the release of stored potential. It is associated with heat, destruction, and the rapid alteration of state from one form to another. Unlike earth, which preserves, or air, which moves, fire consumes and remakes. It has demonstrated heightened activity in the presence of strong emotional or arcane stimuli, suggesting a link between this component and the amplification of internal force. Subjects exposed to its influence often exhibit increased volatility, particularly when already animated by external means.

---

#### Role in Soul Manipulation

The role of the ignic segment within the tetrahedral system appears to be that of amplification. It does not initiate the movement of the soul, nor does it anchor it, but instead intensifies its presence within a host. In reanimated entities, this results in heightened awareness, aggression, and the capacity for directed action beyond simple compulsion. However, such enhancement comes at the cost of stability, as excessive ignic influence may lead to degradation of the host form or uncontrolled release of energy. The component is therefore most effective when balanced by the stabilizing effects of the other elements.

---

#### Magical Philosophy

Fire reveals that the soul is not merely a current, but a force capable of expansion and expression when properly stimulated. It is the principle through which latent potential becomes manifest action. Yet, in this revelation lies inherent danger, for that which is intensified cannot easily be contained. The ignic aspect teaches that power is not created, but drawn forth, often beyond the limits of the vessel that contains it. Mastery, therefore, is not in the act of ignition, but in the restraint of what follows.

## Journal Entry for Water

### ### Water Aspect — Cuprum Unda

#### #### Physical Description

The triangular segment aligned with the aqueous principle is fashioned from copper, its surface bearing a muted, reddish tone that has begun to show signs of natural oxidation. At its center lies a large grey pearl, its surface soft and clouded, lacking the clarity of the others yet possessing a depth that suggests hidden motion beneath stillness. The triangle is inlaid with sapphires, arranged in flowing, wave-like patterns that curve and overlap across the surface. These forms evoke the impression of continuous movement, though rendered in fixed material, as if capturing a moment within an endless cycle.

---

#### #### Thematic Observations

The aqueous aspect governs continuity, memory, and the preservation of state across time. It is associated with blood, tide, and the unseen currents that persist beneath visible change. Unlike fire, which transforms abruptly, water alters gradually, reshaping without destruction. It has demonstrated a strong resonance with decayed or saturated remains, particularly in environments where moisture is present. Entities influenced by this component often display a form of persistence that resists both collapse and escalation, instead maintaining a steady, unyielding state.

---

#### #### Role in Soul Manipulation

Within the system of soul manipulation, the aqueous segment appears to function as a preserver. It sustains the presence of the anima within a host, preventing both rapid dissipation and uncontrolled intensification. In reanimation, it is particularly effective in maintaining cohesion in deteriorated forms, allowing the soul to persist within matter that would otherwise fail. Unlike earth, which binds, water sustains; unlike air, which moves, water endures. It is the element through which the soul remains present over extended duration.

---

#### #### Magical Philosophy

If the soul is a current, then water is its memory—the persistence of motion even when unseen. It does not compel or restrain, but allows continuity, carrying the essence of what was into what remains. The aqueous principle suggests that the soul is not lost at death, but dispersed, its presence diluted rather than destroyed. Through water, that presence may be gathered, sustained, and made to endure beyond its natural span. Mastery of this element is not domination, but patience, allowing the soul to remain where it would otherwise fade.
# Carrying pieces
Carrying pieces of the Tetrahedron carries consequences to the bearer.
	Carrying 1 piece will drain the life of the bearer.  At the end of each extended rest, the bearer will lose two healing surges.
	Carrying 2 pieces will drain the strength of the bearer.  At the end of an extended rest, the bearer is weakened until the next extended rest
	Carrying 3 pieces will sap the mind of the bearer.  At the end of an extended rest, the bearer will suffer a -5 to all skill and ability checks.
	Carrying all 4 pieces will sap the bearer's will. At the end of an extended rest, the bearer will suffer a -2 to all defenses.

## Enchaînement de l’Anima - Trap the Soul

### Type
Soul Binding
### Tone
Grim, Violent
### Casting Time
~3 rounds
### Elements
All (Balanced)
### Description
Placing the assembled tetrahedron on a dying creature absorbs and captures its soul, killing it instantly.
### Incantation
Anima morientis, par droit ancien je t’enchaîne—  
per tetraedrum totum, vinculum aeternum!  
Terra te retinet, Aer te ligat, Ignis te purgat, Aqua te claudit—  
Orgone Vontinere, absorbe et consume—  
anima, cede, et in aeternum contine!
### Translation
Soul of the dying, by ancient right I bind you—  
through the whole tetrahedron, an eternal chain.  
Earth holds you, Air binds you, Fire purifies you, Water encloses you.  
Orgone Vontinere, absorb and consume—  
yield, and be contained forever.

## Concordia Mortis et Ligamen Aeternum - Reassembling the Tetrahedron
### Type
Artifact Ritual (Reassembly)

### Tone
Deceptive, Horrific, Inevitable

### Casting Time
~10–15 minutes (multi-caster ritual)
### Elements
All (Unified)
### Participants
6 required (unknowing sacrifices)
### Description
Six participants perform a cooperative ritual to reassemble the tetrahedron.  
Unbeknownst to them, their souls are consumed and used as binding forces, each forming one of the six edges of the tetrahedron.  
The bodies perish, and the souls are eternally bound within the artifact, holding it together.

### Incantation
Sex voces conveniunt—par volonté commune, nous unissons.  
Non ut moriamur—sed ut formam complétons.

Per fragmenta dispersa, tetraedrum vocamus—  
quod divisum est, nunc conjungitur.

Terra stabilit—fundamentum sine motu—  
ossa et essentia, ad puncta ligantur.

Aqua fluat—inter nodos, inter vincula—  
quod separatum est, nunc continuatur.

Ignis ascendit—per lineas invisibiles—  
energia ligat, et non solvit.

Aer circumdat—spiritus inter nos transit—  
unum ex multis, et multi in uno.

Per orgonem et nomen Vontinere,  
forma completa, potentia integra—  
tetraedrum surgat!

Ligamen nascitur—  
et non frangitur.

Quid… accidit…?

Non corpus—  
sed anima…

Terra retinet—  
Aer trahit—  
Ignis consumit—  
Aqua claudit—

Non sumus… participantes—

sumus… vincula.

Non—  
non hoc…

Per sacrificium ignotum—  
per animas sex—  
forma clauditur—  
et manet.

Tetraedrum completum est.

### Translation
Six voices gather, believing they unite willingly.  
They call the tetrahedron to form, joining what was broken.

Each element binds the structure:  
Earth stabilizes, Water connects, Fire energizes, Air unifies.

The form completes, the power awakens.

Then realization comes—too late.

It is not the body being taken… but the soul.

They are not participants.  
They are the bindings.

Through six souls, the structure is sealed.  
The tetrahedron is complete—and remains.

### Notes
- Each of the six souls becomes one edge of the tetrahedron
- Souls are permanently bound; no known method of release
- Participants are not aware of the true cost before completion
- The shift in the incantation marks the moment of realization
- Often recorded incompletely in surviving texts
