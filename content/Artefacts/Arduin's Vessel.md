Historically known as the Pale Wake, Arduin's Vessel, at first glance, appears to be a 60' sailing yacht, designed for luxury sailing. Quickly though, it is clearly fueled by magic. All controls for the boat, including the sail controls, can be controlled via magic at the helm. To unlock the controls, you must be in possession of Arduin's trinket, a fist sized golden emblem of a helm wheel and anchor. A competent knowledge and experience of sailing is also required to operate the sailing controls effectively.

There are two additional controls as well, one for alternative throttle, which allows for propulsion without the sails, but only at 1/2 speed compared to full sailing.

The second controls are for submersion. Provided the sails are all retracted, the controls can be engaged, the mast fold down, and the top deck seals (glass or force). The ship can then dive, and be navigated underwater. It has a maximum dive time underwater of 3 days, due to air. It takes one hour per day underwater to refresh the air reserves, though this can also be done in caves or natural air pockets underwater, provided there is enough room to surface.

Should the ship be attuned, the ship can be summoned through (interplanar) portals it can create. Should the ship be repaired, it can also be used to navigate the Astral sea.
