# Molrenai - Forge City of the Shadowfell  
  
Molrenai is a vast industrial city built into the side of an active—yet unnaturally stable—volcano within the Shadowfell.  
  
It is a place of fire and ash, where molten rivers fuel endless forges, and the rigid caste system ensures that most who are born in the depths will die there.  
  
---  
  
## Overview  
  
- Location: Shadowfell  
- Nature: Volcanic forge-city  
- Economy: Smithing, metallurgy, infernal craft  
- Notable Feature: Lava flows harnessed for industry  
  
---  
  
## The Volcano  
  
Molrenai is constructed along the slopes of a massive volcano.  
  
- The volcano remains **perpetually active**, but controlled  
- Lava flows are diverted into **forge channels and industrial arteries**  
- Waterfalls of magma cascade through the lower tiers  
  
### Stygian Stone  
  
The city’s stone is infused with the **icy essence of Stygia**, creating a supernatural balance:  
  
- Suppresses extreme volcanic heat  
- Allows habitation near active lava  
- Creates an eerie contrast: *cold stone, burning surroundings*  
  
---  
  
## The Tiers of Molrenai  
  
Molrenai is rigidly stratified, both physically and socially.  
  
### Upper Tiers – The Artisan Lords  
  
- Master smiths, enchanters, and elite artisans  
- Lavish (by Shadowfell standards) estates and workshops  
- Control contracts, trade, and production  
- Guard their knowledge jealously  
  
---  
  
### Middle Tiers – The Working Class  
  
- Journeyman smiths, laborers, and overseers  
- Maintain the forges and infrastructure  
- Slight upward mobility—rare, but possible  
- Often serve as enforcers of the caste system  
  
---  
  
### Lower Tiers – The Ashborn  
  
- Miners and ore-haulers  
- Live in soot-choked, ash-covered districts  
- Constant exposure to heat, toxins, and shadow corruption  
- Short life expectancy  
  
> Most are born here, live here, and die here.  
  
---  
  
## The Shadowdark Mines  
  
Beneath Molrenai lie vast tunnels descending into the Shadowdark.  
  
- Primary source of iron and rare materials  
- Dangerous, unstable, and often haunted  
- Workers face:  
- Cave-ins  
- Shadow-creatures  
- Toxic fumes  
- Supernatural corruption  
  
---  
  
## Society & Culture  
  
- **Rigid caste system** — upward movement is nearly impossible  
- **Merit is secondary to birth**  
- Wealth and power are hoarded by upper tiers  
- Religion and superstition often tied to:  
- Fire  
- Industry  
- Infernal or shadow patrons  
  
---  
  
## The Ashborn Reality  
  
Those born in the lower tiers:  
  
- Live short, brutal lives  
- Breathe ash and smoke daily  
- Exist as expendable labor  
- Rarely see the upper city  
  
Hope is not part of their world.
