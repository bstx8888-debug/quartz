Stonefast was once a dwarven emmisary stronghold.  The stronghold was used as a neutral meeitng place by dwarf clans, to either meet with each other or with other races. The Stonefast clan, a cadre of about a dozen dwarves, ran the diplomatic enclave.  Their lineage could be traced back 7 generations , when the Goznin Mudhood killed an elder dragon.  Legend says he used a catapult trap to decimate the dragon, then took his treasure, and took the name Stonefast as a refernce to his great deed.  Their most recent patriarch, Hemric Stonefast, cared little for politics, and enjoyed the life his family carved from the mountains.   Repeated attempts to overtake the stronghold were rebuffed, but as time went on, aid was less, and in one especially brutal raid, Hemric and several of his kinsmen died.  The few remaining survivors took what possessions they could carry, sealed the entrance, and left to find a new clan to join.  That was 80 years ago.

Approximately 18 months ago, Zanzer Tem located the stronghold of Stonefast, using divination magics.  He was searching for part of the elder dragons treasure, which was left behind when the stronghold was deserted.  By sheer coincidence, the stronghold was below his tower and mine, nearly two miles straight down.  Using his slave labor, enhanced with a little magic, a tunnel was dug to reach the Stronghold, and was completed 12 months ago.  Zanzer was able to explore the sronghold some, and found enough remnants of dragon parts to create a magic item and ritual to attract a live dragon.  He trapped the door so that the dragon may enter, but not leave.  His plan worked well, and 8 months ago, Kamro was trapped.  While within his power to kill the dragon, Zanzer hopes to glean some useful information regarding dragon magic before killing the dragon.  Kamro has been trapped for around 6 months at this point, feeding on "retired" slaves who are sent in as food for the dragon.
